Support email triage

Sorts each customer email by topic and urgency, looks up the order, and drafts a reply for a person to check.

What it does

An online homeware shop gets a steady stream of customer emails: late parcels, refunds, broken items, questions. This Python script reads each new email and asks Claude to sort it into one of five topics with an urgency from 1 to 3. Anything that sounds like a safety problem is always treated as most urgent.

The script then finds the order number, looks up the order, and asks Claude to draft a reply that follows the shop's policy and tone. Urgent emails and high-value orders are flagged for a manager. A person on the support team reviews each draft before it goes out.

Built with: Python (standard library only), the Anthropic API, a CSV order table
